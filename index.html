THE ARTIST

Billie Eilish is an American singer and songwriter known for her unique music, visual style and artistic identity.

GENRE: Pop / Alternative

ROLE: Singer & Songwriter

FACTS ABOUT BILLIE

Billie Eilish became known for her distinctive voice, alternative pop sound and creative music videos.

Her music often explores emotions, personal experiences and different aspects of life.

She is also recognized for her creative fashion, visual concepts and collaborations with her brother FINNEAS.
